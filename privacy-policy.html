Privacy Policy: Helping Hands (customer app)

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Happycrop Agri & Biotech LLP ("we", "us") handles your information when you use the Helping Hands customer app. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you are a service professional using the worker app, see the Worker Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Name, mobile number and email address (when you create an account)
  • Service address and booking details (service type, date, time, notes)
  • Profile information you choose to add
  • Messages you send to our support team

Payment information. Payments are handled by our payment provider [name the payment gateway, e.g. Razorpay]. We do not store your full card, UPI PIN or net banking details. We keep a record of the transaction (amount, date, status).

Collected automatically

  • Device information (model, operating system, app version) and crash/diagnostic data
  • Location, only if you allow it, to find your service address and match you with nearby professionals [confirm whether the app uses location]
  • Notification tokens so we can send booking updates

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and bookings
  • To assign a service professional and share the details needed to complete the job
  • To process payments, refunds and invoices
  • To send booking updates, reminders and important service messages
  • To provide support, resolve disputes and keep the platform safe (fraud prevention)
  • To improve the app and fix problems
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed:

  • Service professionals assigned to your booking: your name, service address, phone number and booking details.
  • Service providers that work for us under confidentiality terms, such as payment processing, cloud hosting, SMS/notification and analytics services [list the real third-party services used].
  • Authorities where the law requires it, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

4. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. We use access controls and reasonable security practices to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so please keep your login details private.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. After you delete your account we delete the personal information we are not required to keep. Records such as transactions, tax and fraud-prevention logs are kept for as long as the law or legitimate business need requires. [confirm retention period]

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access or correct your information by contacting us or editing your profile in the app
  • Turn off location or notification permissions in your phone settings
  • Ask us to delete your account and data: see Delete your account
  • Withdraw consent or raise a complaint using the contact details below

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 18. If we learn that we collected a child's information, we will delete it. [client to confirm minimum age]

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
