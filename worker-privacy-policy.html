Privacy Policy: Helping Hands worker app

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Happycrop Agri & Biotech LLP ("we", "us") handles information of service professionals ("workers") who use the Helping Hands worker app [worker app name]. Customers should read the Customer Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email, profile photo
  • Work details: service categories, skills, experience, service area, availability
  • Verification documents: [list exactly which IDs are collected, e.g. Aadhaar, PAN, police verification. Confirm with client and a lawyer]
  • Payout details: bank account or UPI ID, used only to pay you
  • Job data: jobs accepted, completed or cancelled, ratings and reviews, earnings
  • Location: your location while you are online or on a job, so customers can be matched and kept informed [confirm whether live/background location is used]
  • Device data: device model, OS version, app version, crash logs, notification tokens

2. How we use it

  • To verify your identity and approve your account
  • To send you job requests and manage your schedule
  • To calculate earnings and pay you
  • To keep customers and workers safe, prevent fraud and handle complaints
  • To improve the app and meet legal, tax and accounting duties

3. Who we share it with

  • Customers you are assigned to: your name, photo, phone number, ratings and arrival status.
  • Service providers working for us, such as payment, identity verification, cloud hosting and notification services [list the real third-party services].
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access to documents and payout details is limited to authorised staff.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are an active worker. After you leave or delete your account, we delete what we are not required to keep. Payment, tax and fraud records are kept for the period required by law. [confirm retention period]

6. Your choices and rights

  • Update your profile details in the app or by contacting us
  • Turn off location in your phone settings (you will not receive location-based jobs)
  • Request deletion: see Delete your account

7. Children

Workers must be at least 18 years old.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
