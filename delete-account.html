Delete your Helping Hands account

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This page is for users of the Helping Hands customer app and the Helping Hands worker app, both developed and operated by Happycrop Agri & Biotech LLP. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it.

How to request deletion

Send an email to happycrop.in@gmail.com with the subject Helping Hands - Account Deletion Request and include:

  • Your registered mobile number
  • Your name
  • Which app you used: customer or worker
  • A line saying you want your account deleted

Email a deletion request

To protect your account we may ask for extra details to confirm the request is from you. We will process verified requests within [number of days, e.g. 30].

Data that is deleted

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email, profile information
  • Saved addresses and booking preferences
  • For workers: profile photo, skills, verification documents and payout details we are not required to keep
  • Other personal data linked to your account

Data that may be kept

Some records may be kept where the law or a legitimate need requires it, for only as long as necessary:

  • Payment and transaction records, and tax and accounting records
  • Fraud prevention and security logs
  • Records needed to resolve disputes or meet legal duties

Kept data is not used for normal account services after deletion.

Please note

  • Deletion is permanent. You will lose access and we cannot restore the account.
  • Pending bookings, payouts or refunds should be settled first [confirm].
  • Third-party services we use may keep data under their own legal requirements.
